## ED-324 / ARP6983

An August 2025 SAE G-34 / EUROCAE WG-114 presentation at the FAA AI/ML Technical Exchange gives the committee history (formed 2019; statement of concerns 2021; taxonomy 2024) and a June 2026 target. [C-STAT-001] It describes issue 1 (Draft 7, subject to change) as learning-assurance-focused, limited to non-adaptive supervised ML up to DAL C / AL 3 / SWAL 2, excluding information security and human factors, and unusable if the safety assessment is above IDAL C. [C-STAT-002] It treats a deployed model as software, supporting hardware such as graphics processors as random hardware, and plans reinforcement learning for issue 2; the first ballot drew about 1,800 comments. [C-STAT-003] EUROCAE opened public consultation on 1 August 2025. [C-STAT-004] EUROCAE's September 2026 status page shows a later target of 31 December 2026 ([C-CHAL-005]), so the schedule has slipped.

Boundary: a draft as presented; recheck the published issue before stating final scope.

## EASA NPA 2025-07

Published 10 November 2025, comment deadline extended to 10 March 2026, proposed in response to the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). [C-STAT-005] Current rulemaking status is in [C-STD-002].

## Conventional-baseline dates and characterization

NASA's public account dates DO-178B to December 1992 and DO-178C to December 2011 after seven years of committee work. [C-STAT-006] It describes levels A–E, requirements-based and structural coverage at A/B, the process-quality philosophy, and five tool-qualification levels. [C-STAT-007]

## Road type approval

UK government pages show UN Regulation 157 (automated lane keeping) as an international type-approval regulation that the UK is mandating in GB type approval, with an obligation to accept approvals and a VCA listing review. [C-STAT-008] UNECE and EUR-Lex pages were blocked to automated retrieval on 2026-09-25 (Cloudflare / empty challenge response); EU Implementing Regulation 2022/1426 and the R157 130 km/h amendment are therefore not claimed.
