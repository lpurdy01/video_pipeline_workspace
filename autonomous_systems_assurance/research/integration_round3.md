## Resulting project position

The public material inspected here includes mature methodologies and scoped authority pathways. AMLAS is a public six-stage methodology for a primarily offline-supervised ML component; it explicitly requires complementary system and domain assurance. The MAA route is case-specific military guidance. EASA NPA 2025-07(B) is a substantial civil proposal, not final material, and does not close the project’s highest-consequence case. [C-CHAL-001] [C-CHAL-003] [C-CHAL-004] [C-CHAL-006]

The defensible publication question is therefore: **how can a cross-verifiable evidence graph compose, compare and expose the limits of existing learned-component, system and authority assurance methods for a frozen trained model?** The eventual finding may say only that the dated public search did not identify a final, publicly inspectable civil lifecycle that closes the hardest high-criticality trained-model case. It may not say that no such process exists.
