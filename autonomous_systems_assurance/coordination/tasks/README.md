# Job cards

The coordinator copies [job_template.md](job_template.md) to `<job-id>.md` before assigning a job. Only the coordinator updates these assignment files while workers are active. Workers report progress and completion under their exclusive handoff directory; a job card is not a shared chat log.

Ready-to-assign first research wave:

| Suggested job | Lane | Bounded question | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2026-09-08-road-01` | ROAD | What exactly permits current driverless operations, and what is the Cybercab inquiry investigating? | Primary federal/state records, dates and geographic scope; explicit gaps and claim proposals |
| `2026-09-08-air-01` | AIR | Which GA-ASI and other DAA approval records can be inspected, and what do they authorize? | Equipment/installation/aircraft/operation distinctions; civil/military scope; learned-component evidence or an explicit unknown |
| `2026-09-08-method-01` | METHOD | What would defeat the proposed observation/monitor/recovery architecture? | Primary technical sources, assumptions, shared-input counterexample, alternatives and proposed evidence obligations |

These rows are proposed tasks, not active assignments. The paper and video owners can independently refine their structure from the existing brief while the research wave runs, if enough sessions are available. They should mark draft factual placeholders and propose new claims rather than assuming research outcomes.

Follow-up challenge jobs should be assigned to a different reviewer/session where practical. They write to a new explicitly owned handoff directory. A reviewer may propose edits but does not rewrite the author's lane files during review.
