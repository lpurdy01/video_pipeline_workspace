# Open tensions

These are challenge prompts, not settled empirical results.

1. **Traceability and learned behavior.** Can explicit dataset and objective traceability support the necessary safety claims, and which claims still lack a convincing means of assurance?
2. **Monitor and perception.** Can an independent safeguard observe the hazard the advanced system misses? Which failures are shared? [C-016]
3. **Interpretability and coverage.** Does finding meaningful features establish dependable use across all relevant conditions? What causal intervention would falsify that interpretation? [C-012]
4. **Simplification and rare cases.** Does a smaller model discard uncommon behavior that matters for safety? A compact equation is useful only over its validated domain. [C-011]
5. **Road versus air.** Can the same argument structure apply with different assumptions, or are some necessary recovery guarantees unavailable in one domain?
6. **Multiple models and independent evidence.** Do agreeing reviewers merely inherit the author's framing or repeat the same original source?
7. **Operational permission and demonstrated safety.** Which evidence is public, which may be private to an authority, and which has not been established by the sources inspected?
