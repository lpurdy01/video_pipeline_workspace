Source cards and review limits

These cards link the core factual statements to primary or original research sources. They summarize the project’s scope judgment; use the linked original document for authoritative wording. They are a static reader aid; the generated compiler report is the source of live review-gate status. No human disposition exists yet.

Review state
Model-review refresh is required whenever a manifested claim use changes. Some public-page and scope-only source records retain limited captured context. The live compiler report labels those packages; its completion percentage is review-gate status only, never a safety or certification score.
[C-002] U.S. TSO authorization is distinct from installation/use approval

Project-captured FAA authorization material, represented in the source registry as a limited U.S. approval-scope claim.

Used for: the narrow distinction between equipment authorization and installation or operational use approval.

Limit: local context is short-excerpt only and the current source-support package remains limited. It does not establish an approval chain for a particular DAA product.

[C-TRAIN-001] AMLAS data requirements for the vision task

AMLAS v1.1, University of York, PDF pp. 21–23 (printed pp. 20–22).

Used for: data relevance, completeness, accuracy, balance, installed sensor viewpoint, lighting and partial-occlusion labels.

Limit: these are component-method requirements and examples, not proof that the hypothetical road or air data meet them.

[C-TRAIN-002] AMLAS model learning, overfitting and leakage

AMLAS v1.1, PDF pp. 33–35 (printed pp. 32–34).

Used for: overfitting, simulator-artifact example, development log, candidate choices and internal-test leakage.

Limit: the examples identify failure modes; they do not diagnose any real model or show that a specific regularization or split prevents them.

[C-TRAIN-003] AMLAS independent challenge and verification

AMLAS v1.1, PDF pp. 26, 40–41 (printed pp. 25, 39–40).

Used for: independent verification data, realistic failure classes, unseen inputs and adverse conditions.

Limit: finite verification sets do not prove universal generalization or a vehicle-level safety result.

[C-TRAIN-004] Underfitting and overfitting: textbook distinction

Deep Learning, Chapter 5, Section 5.2 and Figure 5.3, Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville.

Used for: the typical relation between model capacity, training error and generalization error.

Limit: a teaching diagram, not a diagnostic proof for a specific vision model or a safety acceptance threshold.
