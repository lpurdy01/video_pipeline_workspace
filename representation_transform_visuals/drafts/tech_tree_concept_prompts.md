# Tech Tree Concept Art Prompts

These prompts are for exploratory concept art only. The output should inform a Manim implementation, not replace it.

## Shared Context

Create concept art for a public educational video about representation transforms. The visual should feel like a mathematical skill tree: representation transforms unlock practical capabilities. The center is only a visual origin, not a technology. Category wedges branch outward from the center. The frontier is an outer fog-of-war layer, not a normal category.

The categories are:

- Classical signal and media transforms: Fourier transform, frequency-domain view, signal processing, wireless communication, compressed audio, spectral imaging, DCT, JPEG, video codecs.
- Dynamics, stability, and control: Laplace transform, transfer functions, stability/control, feedback controllers, brushless motors, flight/robotics stability, state-space models, neural SSMs.
- Learned representation spaces: neural networks, embeddings, transformers, LLMs, contrastive multimodal spaces, autoencoders, neural codecs, audio tokens, speech/audio agents, multimodal tools.
- Language, code, and verification: software as formal representation, expert systems, blackboard architectures, formal verifiers, code agents, research assistants, V&V/evidence systems, reliable agent workflows.
- Outer frontier layer: transform-native organizations, self-inhabiting compute, autonomous experiment loops, discrete-event world models, closed-loop R&D.

Visual style: dark high-contrast mathematical atlas, 3Blue1Brown-inspired clarity, crisp neon geometry, clean labels as small placeholder blocks, no fantasy ornament, no cartoon characters, no corporate dashboard, no generic sci-fi city. Use the concept art to solve layout and structure.

## Prompt 1: Circular Radial Wedge Map

Design a circular radial skill-tree map for the representation-transform tech tree. A tiny blank origin sits in the center with thin lines to the first real nodes. Four resized category wedges radiate outward: signal/media transforms, dynamics/control, learned representation spaces, and language/code/verification. Branches grow from inner foundational nodes to outer capabilities. Fourier and DCT share one wedge but are distinct branches; Laplace has its own branch in the dynamics wedge. Neural SSMs form a highlighted cross-wedge bridge from state-space models to transformer/LLM sequence models. The frontier appears as a faint outer fog ring beyond all wedges, with speculative nodes barely visible. Make the topology clear enough that Manim could reproduce it with arcs, radial edges, labels, and staged reveals.

## Prompt 2: Circular 3D Orbital Skill Tree

Design a 3D radial/orbital concept for the representation-transform tech tree. Imagine the researched technologies as a flat or slightly curved glowing disk divided into wedges, with branch nodes rising subtly in depth as they become more modern. Fourier, DCT, Laplace, neural networks, and software formalisms start near the center. Capabilities spiral outward along wedge lanes. Cross-category convergence edges are luminous elevated bridges, especially state-space models to neural SSMs to LLMs. The frontier is not a wedge; it is a translucent outer shell or high mist layer surrounding the disk, suggesting unrevealed research territory. The image should be useful for designing a Manim 3D camera move: readable silhouettes, clean depth layers, no cluttered text, strong spatial organization.

## Prompt 3: Rectangular Research Atlas Board

Design a rectangular research-atlas version of the representation-transform tech tree. Use a dark blueprint-like board with four horizontal or diagonal lanes for the categories: signal/media transforms, dynamics/control, learned representation spaces, and language/code/verification. Nodes progress from left/center foundations toward right/outer capabilities. Fourier, DCT, and Laplace are distinct early nodes with branches to concrete technologies. Neural SSMs are a prominent bridge between the dynamics lane and learned representation lane. The frontier is shown as a separate dim region beyond the right edge or top-right boundary, like fog past a map border, not as a normal lane. The design should reveal whether a rectangular Manim layout would be clearer than a circular skill tree for narration.
