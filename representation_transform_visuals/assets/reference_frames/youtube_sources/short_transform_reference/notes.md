## Takeaways For Our Scene

- Start concrete: show words as tokens first, then numerical columns, then a 3D vector space.
- Keep the actual vector math on screen, but sparse and large.
- Relationship vectors should be colored consistently and animated by copying the arrow, not only by moving points.
- Use the source's grammar, not its exact examples. For our video, the clearer original scene is:

```text
king - man + woman ~= queen
```

or geometrically:

```text
vector(man -> king) copied onto woman lands near queen
```

- Use a caveat label: `illustrative projection, not an exact guarantee`.
