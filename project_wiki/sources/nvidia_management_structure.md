## Key Ideas

Huang reportedly manages ~60 direct reports — far exceeding the 5–7 typical for executives at companies of comparable size (~30,000+ employees). He has deliberately eliminated middle management layers and holds no one-on-one meetings. Direct reports are senior and largely self-coordinating; Huang's role is direction-setting, not task-checking. This produces a very flat organization relative to headcount and output.

Contrasted with a DO-178C engineering organization where a DER may have 3–5 direct reports and is responsible for inspecting each artifact individually — the structural difference is the depth of per-artifact inspection required.
