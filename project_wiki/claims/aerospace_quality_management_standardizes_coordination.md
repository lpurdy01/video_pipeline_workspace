# Aerospace Quality Management Standardizes Coordination

## Claim

Aerospace quality management standards such as AS9100/9100 provide an organizational coordination pattern for consistent quality processes, supply-chain alignment, and auditability.

## Status

Candidate. Supported by public IAQG, SAE, and NASA metadata/summaries, not by the licensed AS9100D text.

## Supporting Sources

- IAQG public 9100 overview: 9100 standardizes quality management system requirements for aviation, space, and defense organizations and can be used across supply-chain levels.
- SAE AS9100D metadata: AS9100D includes ISO 9001:2015 requirements plus additional aviation, space, and defense requirements; requirements are complementary to customer/statutory/regulatory requirements.
- NASA SMA article: NASA adopted AS9100 into its quality policy in 2005 and continued through the D revision in 2016.

## Challenges or Uncertainty

- The AS9100D standard itself is licensed. The whitepaper should cite only public summaries unless licensed text is supplied.
- AS9100 is not a software-specific verification standard; it should not be used as evidence for DO-178C-style software traceability.
