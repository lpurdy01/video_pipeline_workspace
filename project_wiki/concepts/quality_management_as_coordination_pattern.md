# Quality Management as Coordination Pattern

## Definition

Quality management systems define organization-level processes for consistent work, supplier coordination, audits, corrective action, and conformity to customer, statutory, and regulatory requirements.

## Why It Matters

The verification compiler whitepaper is not only about algorithms. It is about disciplined coordination among units of intelligence. AS9100-style quality management provides an organizational analogue for process control, auditability, and cross-supplier consistency.

## Source Support

- IAQG public materials describe 9100 as standardizing quality management system requirements across aviation, space, defense, and supply-chain levels.
- SAE metadata describes AS9100D as ISO 9001:2015 plus additional aviation, space, and defense requirements, complementary to customer and regulatory requirements.
- NASA SMA notes NASA's adoption of AS9100 into its quality policy baseline and the transition toward IA9100 naming.
